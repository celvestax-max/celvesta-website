What is inside the switch.

Celvesta runs on a proven open-source carrier switch, so you get the tools wholesale carriers rely on every day.

Routing and call control

  • Least-cost routing with quality checks on answer rate and call duration
  • Automatic re-routing to the next vendor when a call fails, invisible to your customer
  • Time-based routing for peak and off-peak rules
  • Tag-based routing for origin-based rules and billing
  • Blacklists and number translation on source and destination
  • Route simulation to test a change before it goes live

Billing and rates

  • Real-time billing for wholesale transit and prepaid or postpaid retail
  • Flexible rate plans and routing policies built for profit
  • Every call written to the record as it happens
  • Separate customer and vendor accounts in one system
  • Customer top-ups by card (Stripe) and USDT on Business and Carrier plans

Reporting and CDRs

  • Searchable call history and CDR export
  • Customer and vendor traffic reports
  • Interval and custom reports for your own questions
  • Routing failure and answer-rate performance views
  • Media quality statistics and login attempt logs

Security and compliance

  • STIR/SHAKEN caller ID authentication support
  • Blocking of unwanted sources by number or range
  • Number portability lookups to route ported numbers correctly
  • Customer authentication by IP address or login

Media and interconnect

  • Session border controller for carrier interconnects
  • Codec handling and transcoding between networks
  • Load and capacity limits per customer and vendor
  • Microsoft Teams Direct Routing support

Automation and portals

  • REST API to configure and provision without manual work
  • Branded customer portal for your clients
  • Branded admin panel for your team
  • Managed updates so you always run a current version

Some features need setup on your side, for example STIR/SHAKEN certificates and number portability database access. We help you configure them.
